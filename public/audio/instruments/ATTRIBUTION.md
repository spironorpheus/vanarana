# Sample provenance

These instrument samples are derived from the CC0 1.0 public-domain sample libraries published by Versilian Studios and contributors. No TimGM6mb or other GPL soundfont samples are included.

- VSCO 2 Community Edition: https://versilian-studios.com/vsco-community/
- Source repository: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
- VCSL: https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
- License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Piano: VCSL Steinway B grand piano, close microphone, no-sustain articulation. Strings: VSCO violin section sustained vibrato. Cello: VSCO cello section sustained vibrato. Winds: VSCO sustained oboe and F horn.

Source license texts are included alongside this file. Exact repository commits, source URLs, source pitches, transpositions, and measured output data are in provenance.json. VSCO orchestral filenames use an octave convention one lower than scientific pitch notation; this was checked against actual spectral pitches. VCSL piano uses scientific pitch notation.

Derived samples were pitch-shifted by offline resampling to the exact required MIDI pitches, converted to mono 22050 Hz PCM16, trimmed to 2.5 seconds, and given short boundary fades. One consistent gain per instrument bank caps its loudest sample at 0.65 full scale, preserving relative sample levels. Original source recordings remain available at the pinned URLs. Runtime playback performs no composition or pitch generation.
